Run the rescue workflow

01

Enter an offer

Start with bakery surplus, urgent chilled food or a full-lot capacity stress case. Check quantity, storage, pickup origin and the donor’s actual deadline.

02

Review the evidence

Inspect the proposal, rejected candidates and unresolved facts. A no-match is a valid result. Use the map to explore approximate locations and neighborhood context.

03

Confirm the handoff

Review and copy the Match Brief or outreach draft. Confirm recipient acceptance, actual address, operational capacity and pickup timing before arranging delivery.

Know what the demo establishes

Real retrieval. Explicit constraints.

The core service uses an ADK/Gemini agent, MCP Toolbox and BigQuery vector search. Physical feasibility checks and a final deadline recheck produce the grounded brief. A successful run demonstrates the software path; it does not confirm a recipient’s live availability.

Source limits stay visible.

Operational recipient profiles are generated or planted demo data. Census indicators describe neighborhoods, not individual need; map locations are approximate. Directions open separately. FoodKeeper is consumer quality guidance and does not certify safety or extend a deadline.

The app sends no outreach, makes no reservation and dispatches no delivery. No measured food-rescue or equity improvement is claimed.
